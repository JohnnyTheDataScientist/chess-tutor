How these models work

Each model is a neural network that looks at the board once and picks the move it rates highest. There is no search: it does not calculate ahead, so it plays instantly and can miss tactics a calculating engine would see.

The ratings are measured in games against Stockfish's limited-strength levels at 2 minutes + 1 second. They are on Stockfish's scale, which is anchored to engine rating lists, so read them as a rough guide, not as a human rating.

To keep games from repeating, the model picks among its top choices in its first three moves. After that it always plays its first choice.

Nothing you play is uploaded. The model files are downloaded once and run on your device.
